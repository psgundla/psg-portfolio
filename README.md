## The meaning behind my logo

My logo is a **PG monogram** woven together with the Sanskrit **“swa”** from Swaroop. Pranav and Swaroop also evoke **Om** for me — a connection carried into the design.

The large **G arc** represents **Gundla**, my family name. It embraces every element, bringing them together and making me whole.
